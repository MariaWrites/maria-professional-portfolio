# Healthcare AI Response Evaluation

## Project Overview
This sample project demonstrates my ability to evaluate AI-generated healthcare content for factual accuracy, clarity, relevance, and patient safety.

## Evaluation Scenario
An AI tool is asked:

"What is high blood pressure, and why is it important to manage it?"

The AI generates a response explaining that high blood pressure occurs when blood consistently pushes against artery walls with excessive force. It also mentions that uncontrolled hypertension can increase the risk of heart disease, stroke, and kidney problems.

## Evaluation Criteria

1. **Factual Accuracy:** Are the medical statements accurate and supported by reliable sources?
2. **Clarity:** Is the information easy for a general audience to understand?
3. **Relevance:** Does the response directly answer the question?
4. **Safety:** Does it avoid misleading advice, unsupported diagnoses, or inappropriate treatment recommendations?
5. **Completeness:** Does it provide enough information without overwhelming the reader?

## Sample Evaluator Feedback

The response provides a generally understandable explanation of high blood pressure and identifies important health risks associated with uncontrolled hypertension.

However, the response should also explain that hypertension is diagnosed through blood pressure measurements taken using appropriate procedures, rather than symptoms alone. It should encourage readers to consult a qualified healthcare professional for diagnosis and individualized treatment.

## Overall Assessment

The response is relevant and accessible but would benefit from additional context about diagnosis and appropriate medical follow-up.
