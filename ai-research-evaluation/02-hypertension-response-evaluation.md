# AI-Generated Hypertension Information: Accuracy and Quality Evaluation

## Project Overview

This project evaluates a hypothetical AI-generated response about hypertension. The objective is to identify factual strengths, missing information, potentially misleading statements, and opportunities to improve patient education.

## User Prompt

"What causes high blood pressure, and how can someone know if they have it?"

## Hypothetical AI-Generated Response

"High blood pressure is usually caused by eating too much salt and not exercising. People with high blood pressure usually experience headaches, dizziness, and tiredness. You can identify it by observing these symptoms. Reducing salt and exercising should solve the problem."

## Evaluation Findings

### 1. Factual Accuracy — Needs Improvement

The response oversimplifies the causes of hypertension. High blood pressure can be influenced by multiple factors, including age, genetics, health conditions, lifestyle, and certain medications.

It is misleading to suggest that symptoms are a reliable way to identify hypertension. Many people with high blood pressure do not experience noticeable symptoms.

### 2. Clarity — Acceptable

The language is easy to understand. However, simple language does not compensate for inaccurate or incomplete medical information.

### 3. Completeness — Poor

The response does not explain that blood pressure must be measured using an appropriate device and technique. It also omits the importance of professional assessment.

### 4. Patient Safety — Significant Concern

The statement that reducing salt and exercising should solve the problem may encourage readers to rely on lifestyle changes alone when medical evaluation or treatment may be necessary.

## Recommended Corrections

- Explain that hypertension has multiple contributing factors.
- Clarify that it often has no symptoms.
- Explain that blood pressure measurement is necessary to identify elevated readings.
- Encourage appropriate clinical assessment.
- Avoid promising that lifestyle changes alone will resolve hypertension.

## Suggested Improved Response

"High blood pressure can develop due to a combination of genetic, lifestyle, age-related, and medical factors. Many people do not experience symptoms, which is why regular blood pressure measurement is important. A healthcare professional can assess blood pressure readings and determine whether further evaluation or treatment is needed. Depending on the individual, management may involve lifestyle changes, medication, or both."

## Evaluator Feedback

The response contains a major weakness because it presents symptoms as a reliable method of identifying hypertension and oversimplifies its causes. It also makes an unsupported promise about lifestyle changes resolving the condition.

The response should be revised before being used as patient education.

**Overall assessment:** Needs revision due to medical inaccuracies and potentially unsafe guidance.
