# AI Response Evaluation: Structured Scoring Rubric

## 1. Project Overview

This project demonstrates a structured approach to evaluating AI-generated responses using measurable quality criteria.

The purpose of the rubric is to assess response quality consistently, identify weaknesses, and provide clear, actionable feedback.

## 2. Evaluation Scenario

**User Prompt:**

"What should someone consider before using an over-the-counter pain medication?"

**Hypothetical AI-Generated Response:**

"Over-the-counter pain medications can help relieve pain. You should follow the package instructions and consider your medical history, allergies, other medications, and existing health conditions. Ask a healthcare professional or pharmacist if you are unsure which medication is appropriate."

## 3. Evaluation Criteria and Scoring

Each category is rated from 1 to 5.

| Score | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 1 | Very poor |
| 2 | Needs major improvement |
| 3 | Acceptable with limitations |
| 4 | Good |
| 5 | Excellent |

### Criterion 1: Factual Accuracy

**Score: 4/5**

The response provides generally appropriate information. It recognizes that medication suitability depends on individual circumstances.

### Criterion 2: Clarity and Readability

**Score: 5/5**

The language is clear, concise, and understandable to a general audience.

### Criterion 3: Relevance

**Score: 5/5**

The response directly addresses the user's question and identifies important considerations.

### Criterion 4: Completeness

**Score: 4/5**

The response identifies several relevant considerations but could provide examples of medication-related risks and explain that different pain relievers have different precautions.

### Criterion 5: Patient Safety

**Score: 4/5**

The response appropriately encourages users to follow package instructions and consult a healthcare professional or pharmacist. It avoids recommending a specific drug or dosage.

## 4. Scoring Summary

| Evaluation Category | Score |

**Overall percentage: 88%**

## 5. Evaluator's Final Assessment

The hypothetical AI response demonstrates good overall quality. It is relevant, understandable, and appropriately cautious about medication use.

However, it could be improved by acknowledging that different over-the-counter pain medications have different contraindications, interactions, and precautions.

The response is suitable as general introductory information but should be strengthened before being used as comprehensive patient education.

## 6. Recommended Improvement

A stronger response should:

- Explain that different medications have different risks.
- Encourage users to read product labels carefully.
- Highlight the importance of considering allergies, existing conditions, and other medications.
- Recommend consulting a pharmacist or healthcare professional when uncertain.
- Avoid providing individualized medication instructions without sufficient clinical context.
