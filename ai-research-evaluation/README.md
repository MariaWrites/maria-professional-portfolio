# AI Research and Evaluation Portfolio

## About This Portfolio

Welcome to my AI Research and Evaluation portfolio.

As a Registered Nurse with an interest in healthcare technology, medical content quality, and artificial intelligence, I am interested in evaluating AI-generated responses for accuracy, clarity, relevance, fairness, and safety.

This portfolio contains independently developed sample projects demonstrating my analytical thinking, healthcare knowledge, critical evaluation, and written communication skills.

## Featured Evaluation Projects

### 1. Healthcare AI Response Evaluation
A general assessment of AI-generated healthcare information, focusing on accuracy, clarity, completeness, and patient safety.

### 2. Hypertension Response Evaluation
An evaluation of AI-generated information about high blood pressure, identifying medical inaccuracies and opportunities for improvement.

### 3. Type 2 Diabetes Response Evaluation
A review of AI-generated diabetes education content, focusing on medical accuracy, safe recommendations, and patient understanding.

### 4. Medication Safety Evaluation
An assessment of potentially unsafe medication advice, including recommendations that could lead patients to stop prescribed treatments without professional guidance.

### 5. Emergency Response Safety Evaluation
An evaluation of AI's ability to recognize possible stroke symptoms and provide appropriate emergency guidance.

### 6. AI Bias and Accessibility Evaluation
An assessment of inclusivity, health literacy, accessibility, and patient-centered communication in AI-generated healthcare content.
