Knowing and maintaining a healthy blood pressure is important for good cardiovascular health. Understanding the meaning of the systolic and diastolic numbers and maintaining regular blood pressure checks, healthy lifestyle and medical advice can help lower the potential for complications.

Don't forget that HBP can occur without any warning signs. Ongoing monitoring and early medical intervention are important.
