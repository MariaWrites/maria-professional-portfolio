# General Proofreading Sample

## Project Overview

This sample demonstrates the ability to identify and correct grammar, spelling, punctuation, capitalization, and sentence construction errors.

### Original Text (Before Editing)

Many people wants to improve their writing skills but they does not know where to start. Writing clearly are important because it helps readers understands information without getting confused.

A good writer should always checks their work before submitting it. Mistakes such as incorrect punctuation, spelling errors and repeated words can makes a document look unprofessional.

### Edited Text (After Proofreading)

Many people want to improve their writing skills, but they do not know where to start. Writing clearly is important because it helps readers understand information without becoming confused.

A good writer should always check their work before submitting it. Mistakes such as incorrect punctuation, spelling errors, and repeated words can make a document look unprofessional.

## Key Corrections Made

| Issue | Correction |
|---|---|
| Subject-verb agreement | "Many people wants" → "Many people want" |
| Verb agreement | "They does not" → "They do not" |
| Sentence structure | "Writing clearly are" → "Writing clearly is" |
| Verb form | "Readers understands" → "Readers understand" |
| Punctuation | Added commas where needed |
| Verb form | "Can makes" → "Can make" |

## Skills Demonstrated

- Grammar and spelling correction
- Punctuation improvement
- Subject-verb agreement
- Verb tense consistency
- Sentence clarity
- Attention to detail
