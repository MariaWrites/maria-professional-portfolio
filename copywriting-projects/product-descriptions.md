**Project:** GlowNaturals Product Collection  
**Industry:** Beauty and Skincare  
**Content Type:** E-commerce Product Descriptions

*This is an original fictional portfolio project created to demonstrate e-commerce product description writing, persuasive communication, and marketing skills. GlowNaturals is a fictional brand.*

## Project Objective

Create engaging and persuasive product descriptions that communicate product features, highlight customer benefits, and encourage purchasing decisions.

## PRODUCT 1: Hydrating Facial Cleanser

**Product Name:** GlowNaturals Daily Hydrating Facial Cleanser

**Short Description:**

A gentle daily facial cleanser designed to remove everyday impurities while leaving your skin feeling fresh, clean, and comfortable.

**Full Product Description:**

Give your skincare routine a refreshing start.

GlowNaturals Daily Hydrating Facial Cleanser is designed for people who want a simple, refreshing cleansing experience without an unnecessarily complicated routine.

Its gentle cleansing formula helps remove everyday dirt, excess oil, and impurities while leaving your skin feeling clean and comfortable.

Whether you're getting ready for the day or winding down at night, make cleansing a moment of care.

**Key Features:**
- Gentle daily cleansing.
- Helps remove everyday impurities.
- Leaves skin feeling refreshed.
- Suitable for a simple morning and evening skincare routine.

**Call to Action:** Add to Your Skincare Routine.

## Copywriting Strategy

**Target Audience:** Adults interested in skincare and personal care products.

**Brand Voice:** Friendly, confident, approachable, and persuasive.

**Writing Approach:**
- Lead with customer needs and interests.
- Highlight product benefits rather than listing features alone.
- Use clear and engaging language.
- Make descriptions easy to scan.
- Include persuasive calls to action.
- Maintain a consistent brand voice.
